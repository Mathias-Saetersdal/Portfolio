About

I am an interaction designer in Bergen, with a master's in interaction design from NTNU and a bachelor's in digital culture from the University of Bergen.

My field is accessibility. As a student assistant I taught students accessibility and WCAG.

I write my own frontend. This site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no framework and no build step. Every color is measured against the contrast requirements, and the measurement sits as a comment in the CSS.
